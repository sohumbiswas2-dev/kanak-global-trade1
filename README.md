# Kanak Global Trade

Design a homepage for "Kanak Industries FZCO", a Dubai-based B2B international trading company (est. 2012, Jumeirah Lakes Towers). This is a company-profile and quotation-enquiry site, NOT e-commerce: no prices, cart, or login.

Brand: orange (#E8590C as placeholder) and charcoal (#2B2B2B), clean white background, professional sans-serif typography. Leave a logo placeholder in the header; I will insert the real logo.

Header: Home | About | Products (dropdown) | Industries | Request a Quote (highlighted button) | Contact.

Sections:
1. Hero: headline "International Trading & Industrial Supply Solutions", subline "Steel, metallurgical materials, industrial equipment and coffee — connecting business buyers with sourcing and supply opportunities across global markets." Two buttons: Request a Quote, Contact Us.
2. Short company intro (established 2012, Dubai, serving Africa, Middle East, Europe, Asia).
3. Three business divisions: Steel & Metallurgical Materials | Industrial Plant Equipment & Spare Parts | Fluid-Control, Electrical & Coffee.
4. Featured categories: 7 large image cards: Steel & Basic Steel Products, Coffee, Refractory Materials, Metal Alloys, Industrial Plant Equipment & Spares, Pumps/Engines/Valves & Spares, Electrical Fittings. Each card has a "Request a Quote" link.
5. Markets served strip: Africa, Middle East, Europe, Asia.
6. Footer: address (HDS Tower, Cluster F, Al Thanyah Fifth, JLT, Dubai, UAE), frontdesk@kanak-industries.com, +971 56 645 9911, links.

Style: trustworthy, industrial, uncluttered, large readable cards, mobile-first. Use generic stock-style imagery placeholders. Do not claim current stock or show prices.
